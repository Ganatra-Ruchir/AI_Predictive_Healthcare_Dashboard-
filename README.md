# Signals of Proof — AI in Predictive Healthcare

A plain-English, interactive dashboard paired with a formal IEEE-style evidence review, covering what verified 2024–2026 research actually shows about AI-powered wearables — atrial fibrillation, glucose monitoring, Parkinson's disease, dementia, epilepsy, sleep apnea, mental health, COPD, frailty, federated learning, and human digital twins.

Every number in both the dashboard and the report traces back to a peer-reviewed publication (DOI/PMID), a ClinicalTrials.gov registration, or an official regulatory source (FDA/WHO). No synthetic data, no invented effect sizes.

## Why this exists

Most "AI in healthcare" coverage blends four very different kinds of claim — how accurate a model is in a lab, whether it changes a real clinical outcome, whether it's actually used in practice, and whether regulators have signed off on it — into one confident-sounding headline. This project keeps those four things separate, and refuses to state a number that can't be traced back to its source.
